Biology 2

Steven Cooke Biology

The overlooked role of food and cultural dimensions in recreational fisheries

The authors surveyed 443 shore anglers in 10 cities in the lower Great Lakes about their preferences, behaviours, use of their catch and background. They identified five dimensions of value, including food and cultural dimensions, and sorted anglers into three groups (food-oriented, cultural or community-oriented, and leisure or sport-oriented), which suggests managers should treat food and culture as core parts of recreational fisheries.

Bridging conservation evidence and policy through knowledge co‐production: Perspectives from senior environmental professionals in Canada

The authors interviewed 82 senior environmental professionals in federal, provincial and territorial governments and NGOs across Canada about how they work with partners to produce knowledge that informs policy. Most saw co-production as useful for making research relevant to policy, and they named early relationship-building and sustained resourcing as helpful, and bureaucratic constraints and mismatched timelines as barriers.

Earth Sciences 1

Richard Amos Earth Sciences

Groundwater solution composition effects on copper corrosion in deep geological repositories

Electrochemical experiments and surface analysis were used to compare how copper corrodes in two sodium chloride solutions and in a synthetic version of groundwater from a Canadian Shield repository for spent nuclear fuel. Corrosion depended on oxygen, ionic strength and solution composition, with rates about twice as high at the higher ionic strength, and the groundwater analogue produced lower corrosion rates than the sodium chloride solutions, which matters for how well copper-coated fuel containers are assessed.

Health Sciences 1

Utkarsh Dang Health Sciences

Cortisol Cutoffs for Adrenal Insufficiency Using Elecsys II: A Systematic Review

This systematic review looked at six studies, covering 1799 samples from 430 adult and child patients, that recommended a cortisol cutoff for diagnosing adrenal insufficiency with the Roche Elecsys II assay. The authors suggest a peak cutoff of 405 nmol/L for a standard-dose ACTH test, lower than the older 500 nmol/L threshold, and note that the small number and limited quality of the studies constrain the recommendation.

Neuroscience 1

Amedeo D'Angiulli Neuroscience

Recall of overlearned speech in noise: Developmental ERP findings and an episodic buffer frame for culturally situated brain activity

This perspective paper reanalyzes data from children and young adults who had memorized Quranic passages without fully understanding Arabic, and who picked the missing final word of passages played with or without static noise. Noise reduced accuracy, children responded more slowly and showed larger, more widely spread brain responses, and the authors offer an episodic buffer explanation while describing the findings as preliminary and in need of further testing.

Physics 3

Sangeeta Murugkar Physics

Label-free multimodal nonlinear optical imaging reveals key biomarkers of cellular radiation response

The authors built a label-free imaging platform that combines stimulated Raman scattering and two-photon fluorescence microscopy, and used it on human lung cancer cells given clinical X-ray doses of 2 to 10 Gy. Irradiated cells showed measurable changes that varied with dose and time, including altered lipid and protein signals, a higher optical redox ratio, larger cells and lipid droplets, and more lipid droplets located with mitochondria, which supports using this imaging to assess cellular response to radiation.

Dag Gillberg Physics, Jesse Heilman Physics, Alain Bellerive Physics, Kevin Graham Physics, Thomas Koffas Physics, Manuella Vincter Physics with a large collaboration

Search for charged-lepton-flavour violating $e\mu tq$ interactions at $\sqrt{s}=13$ TeV with the ATLAS detector

Using 140 fb^-1 of proton-proton collision data from the ATLAS detector, the team searched for top-quark processes that would change the type of charged lepton, which the Standard Model does not predict in this form. They saw no significant deviation from the Standard Model and set upper limits on the strength of these interactions and on the rates of the corresponding top-quark decays.
