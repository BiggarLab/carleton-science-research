Steven Cooke Biology

A case study on attempts to remove invasive Largemouth Bass from two small lakes in Québec

Researchers and community partners spent three years removing invasive Largemouth Bass from two small lakes in Gatineau Park, using nest destruction, angling, seining and traps. Active nests in Lac Kingsmere fell from 25 to 0 and catches dropped by over 90% in abundance, but eradication would need several more years of monitoring, and community participation was important for early detection and sustained effort.

Utkarsh Dang Health Sciences

Muscle function, linear growth, and bone health during 30 months of vamorolone, prednisone, and deflazacort treatment for Duchenne muscular dystrophy: a comparative study

This study compared 30 months of vamorolone treatment in boys with Duchenne muscular dystrophy against matched boys on prednisone or deflazacort, the standard corticosteroids, using data from separate non-randomized trials. Motor function did not differ significantly between the drugs, but height stayed stable on vamorolone and fell on the other two, and vertebral fractures were about half as frequent.

Robyn McQuaid Neuroscience

Timing matters: Maternal stress and mental health symptoms from pregnancy to postpartum in shaping infant development

The study followed 146 Pakistani mother-infant pairs from pregnancy to postpartum and linked maternal stress, depressive symptoms and a physiological stress measure to poorer infant fine motor skills. Higher social support after birth weakened the link between prenatal stress and fine motor skills, which suggests that support and lower postpartum stress could help, though the findings are associations.

Dag Gillberg Physics, Jesse Heilman Physics, Alain Bellerive Physics, Kevin Graham Physics, Thomas Koffas Physics, Manuella Vincter Physics with a large collaboration

Search for dark photons from Higgs boson decays in the gluon-gluon fusion channel in proton-proton collisions at $$ \sqrt{s}=13.6 $$ TeV with the ATLAS detector

The ATLAS collaboration searched 135 fb-1 of 2023 and 2024 collision data for Higgs bosons decaying into a photon and a dark photon, using a trigger with lower energy thresholds introduced in 2023. No significant excess over Standard Model predictions was found, so the result sets an upper limit of 1.4% on how often this decay occurs, tightened to 0.9% when combined with earlier Run 2 searches.
