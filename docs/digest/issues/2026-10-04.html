Biology 3

Bruce McKay Biology, David Sabatino Chemistry, Maria DeRosa Chemistrycross-unit

Uncovering the importance of lipofection for aptamer-mediated therapeutic oligonucleotide delivery in a HER2+ SKBR3 breast cancer cell line

The team modified a DNA aptamer that targets the HER2 protein so it carried antisense or siRNA sequences aimed at silencing the cancer survival protein GRP78 in HER2-positive SKBR3 breast cancer cells. The modified aptamer bound to and entered the cells, but the siRNA gene silencing only worked when a Lipofectamine transfection agent was used, so the delivery formulation matters for these genetic therapies. The work was done in a cell line.

Bioorganic & Medicinal Chemistry Letters · Sep 30 · open access
Nicolas Rodrigue Biology

Identifying patterns of selection at synonymous sites in a Pseudomonas phylogeny

The author used a statistical method on sequence comparisons from Pseudomonas bacteria to find DNA changes that do not alter the protein but still appear to be under selection. About 8% of the tested sites in one gene and 7.5% in another were highly constrained, including some already shown by experiments to be beneficial, which suggests these changes may often contribute to adaptation.

Genome Biology and Evolution · Sep 29 · open access
Heath MacMillan Biology

Drivers of insect decline: direct, indirect, and combined pressures

This review sorts the known causes of insect decline using the IPBES assessment framework and attaches confidence statements so policy groups can use the findings directly. Land-use and climate change are well established as drivers, while insecticides, light pollution and the combined effects of multiple stressors are less well understood, though the authors say enough is known to start planning action.

Annals of the Entomological Society of America · Sep 4 · open access

Chemistry 1

Katherine Marczenko Chemistry

Pendant Phosphonium Outperforms Ammonium by Strengthening Local Electric Fields in a Ruthenium Hydrogenation Catalyst

The researcher compared two versions of a ruthenium catalyst for turning CO2 into methanol, one with a pendant phosphonium group and one with an ammonium group. The phosphonium version gave higher turnover and better selectivity for methanol, and calculations indicate it lowers the energy barrier of the rate-limiting step by stabilizing charge more strongly.

ACS Catalysis · Sep 30

Computer Science 3

Nadine Marie Moacdieh Computer Science

Measuring display clutter in different contexts: evidence from performance and attention in websites and maps

Participants searched for targets on websites and maps while the authors compared three computer algorithms that measure display clutter against search time and eye tracking data. Only feature congestion was linked to response time in both websites and maps, and the results offer guidance on which clutter measures and eye tracking metrics to use for each display type.

AbdelRahman Abdou Computer Science

The Silent Spill: Measuring Sensitive Data Leaks Across Public URL Repositories

The authors built an automated system to find sensitive information exposed in URLs published by security scanning services, paste sites and web archives. Applied to about 6.1 million URLs, it flagged 12,331 potential exposures involving authentication, financial, personal and document-related content, which shows that accidental leaks through these services are still happening.

Mathematics & Statistics 1

Dave Campbell Mathematics & Statistics

Signal-Adapted Kernels and Lazy-Evaluation Gaussian Process Regression

The authors developed a kernel for Gaussian process regression that adapts its shape to the data, along with a lazy-evaluation version of the method designed to scale to larger datasets and to keep predictions continuous. They compared the approach with standard methods on rainfall and elevation data, looking at unwanted oscillations and how well the fits follow the contours of the data.

Neuroscience 1

Amedeo D'Angiulli Neuroscience

Pediatric social robotics in ASD: navigating AI blind-spots toward clinical validation

This mini-review looks at three areas of social robots for children with autism that have little validation so far: robots paired with wearable sensors, virtual reality and robot hybrids, and generative AI conversation. The authors conclude these systems cannot yet be supported as primary therapy, and they propose a five-module clinical validation roadmap including standardized outcome measures and multisite randomized trials.

European Journal of Pediatrics · Sep 29 · open access

Physics 2

Dag Gillberg Physics, Jesse Heilman Physics, Alain Bellerive Physics, Kevin Graham Physics, Thomas Koffas Physics with a large collaboration

Search for compressed electroweak SUSY production in final states with two $\tau$-leptons in $\sqrt{s} = 13$ and $13.6$ TeV $pp$ collisions with the ATLAS detector

This ATLAS collaboration paper searches proton-proton collision data from the Large Hadron Collider for supersymmetric particles (tau-sleptons, charginos and neutralinos) in cases where the particle masses are close together. The results are generally consistent with the Standard Model, with a few deviations of 2.1 to 2.4 sigma, and the paper sets mass limits for some scenarios but none for direct tau-slepton production when all channels are combined.

CERN Document Server (European Organization for Nuclear Research) · Sep 29 · large collaboration
Thomas Koffas Physics

Carrier dynamics in neutron irradiated high-quality epitaxial GaN

The authors used three measurement techniques to study how charge carriers recombine and become trapped in high-quality gallium nitride layers before and after neutron irradiation. Carrier lifetime fell from about 200 ns to about 2 ps across the conditions studied as radiation defects increased, and the data point to trapping centres at about 0.3 eV, likely linked to carbon and carbon-oxygen complexes.

Scientific Reports · Sep 29 · open access
