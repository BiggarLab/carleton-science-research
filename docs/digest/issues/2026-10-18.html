Biology 3

Vivian Nguyen Biology

Unlocking the potential of aquatic animal telemetry research to advance management and conservation: Barriers and a blueprint for Great Britain and Ireland

The authors surveyed 53 researchers to map aquatic animal telemetry work in Britain and Ireland and the barriers to using it in management. Seventy-three percent of respondents reported influencing management or policy decisions, and the authors recommend a coordinated research hub, better data sharing, and a permanent, interoperable acoustic receiver array.

Lenore Fahrig Biology

Patch isolation increases with habitat loss, but decreases with habitat fragmentation

The authors reviewed 100 papers and found that 83% of those using distance-based isolation metrics assumed isolation increases with habitat fragmentation, even though geometry says the reverse for a fixed amount of habitat. Using thousands of real forest landscapes, they showed that forest patch isolation declines as fragmentation increases, and they argue that correcting this assumption would help move the fragmentation debate forward.

Earth Sciences 2

Hillary Maddin Earth Sciences

Early tetrapod neck mobility modelled using an extant analogue

The authors tested the axial columns of sturgeon, which are built similarly to those of early tetrapods, to model how far early tetrapod necks could bend and rotate. Most stem tetrapods could move their heads less than 6° sideways and less than 3° up or down, and could not rotate them. Mobility did not change when the head separated from the shoulder girdle, so the authors conclude that neck mobility did not drive that separation and that forelimb-driven locomotion is a better explanation.

Nature · Oct 14 · open access
Richard Amos Earth Sciences

Metal Transport through Century-Old Waste Rock

The authors extracted and analyzed porewater from a waste-rock pile at an abandoned prospecting property that has been in place for about 100 years. They found near-neutral pH, active carbonate buffering, and zinc making up 92% of heavy metals, attributed to weathering of sphalerite. Differences from other waste-rock studies were mostly explained by rock type (sulfide minerals and carbonate content) rather than by the age of the pile.

Health Sciences 1

Francine Darroch Health Sciences

The sustainability of a trauma- and violence-informed physical activity program for women experiencing interpersonal and structural violence

The authors followed a trauma- and violence-informed physical activity program for women at three community sites in Canada for 12 months after delivery passed from the research team to local providers. Fidelity was high (median 91%) and average attendance was 7.6 participants per session. Interviews showed that community delivery is feasible but depends on leadership, a stable facilitator, and dedicated program management roles rather than short-term grant funding.

International Journal of Behavioral Nutrition and Physical Activity · Oct 15 · open access

Neuroscience 1

Amedeo D'Angiulli Neuroscience

Steps to an ecology of brain and cognition: From environmental effects to dynamic neurocognitive adaptation and resilience

This editorial introduces a special issue on physical, social and economic environmental effects on brain and cognition. It draws on the contributions to argue for an ecological view in which neural and cognitive differences are interpreted in light of environmental demands, costs and opportunities for recovery, and it sets priorities for longitudinal, multimodal research.

Physics 1

Dag Gillberg Physics, Jesse Heilman Physics, Alain Bellerive Physics, Thomas Koffas Physics, Manuella Vincter Physics with a large collaboration

Search for a leptoquark in events with a hadronically decaying τ-lepton and missing transverse momentum using pp collisions at s=13TeV with the ATLAS detector

The ATLAS collaboration searched proton-proton collision data from Run 2 of the Large Hadron Collider for signs of a leptoquark, a hypothetical particle, in events with a hadronically decaying tau lepton and missing transverse momentum. No excess above the Standard Model prediction was seen, so the authors set limits on couplings in a benchmark vector-leptoquark model for masses between 1.5 TeV and 3.0 TeV.

Recommended

Clustering method for longitudinal RNA-seq counts, with public code

A mixture model that clusters subjects by multivariate count trajectories over time. It handles irregular timing and covariates. A variational EM algorithm keeps it scalable. Tested in simulations and on COVID-19 neutrophil RNA-seq data.

Route
Partnership. Code is public on GitHub and the preprint is open, so this is a know-how partnership, not a licence.
