Benjamin Woo Journalism and Communication

Making Worlds: For an Ecology of Cultural Production

Benjamin Woo and co-author Miranda Campbell wrote a book arguing that research and policy on culture focus too much on commercial creative industries and overlook the work of amateurs, hobbyists, volunteers, non-profits and countercultural groups. They draw on their own involvement with comics creators, game players, music scenes, zine-makers, artist co-operatives and fan cultures to propose an ecological view of how cultural activity is sustained. It matters because it responds to gig-economy boosterism and widens what counts as culture in research and policy, including the work of people and spaces that are underfunded or at risk of eviction and gentrification.
