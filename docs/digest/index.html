Biology 1

Catherine Cullingham Biology

Effects on Genetic Architecture From Mountain Pine Beetle Mortality in Lodgepole Pine

The authors collected cones from lodgepole pine trees killed by mountain pine beetle and from trees that survived, grew the offspring in a common garden, and genotyped 418 of them at 14,267 loci. They found reduced genetic diversity at most sampled sites and loci that differed between offspring of survivors and of killed trees, which indicates the beetle selects on pine genotype and that regeneration from survivors may increase variation linked to reduced host quality for the beetle.

Molecular Ecology · Nov 1 · open access

Computer Science 1

Lianying Zhao Computer Science

Boosting Automated Vulnerability Condition Classification Through Multi-Source Information Aggregation

The authors built a system that classifies the preconditions and postconditions of software vulnerabilities by combining several databases, including IBM X-Force Exchange and the Red Hat CVE database, instead of relying only on the National Vulnerability Database. It improved F1 scores by 3% to 18% over single-source baselines, with gains up to 69% where the NVD had little detail, and a case study on the Free5GC 5G core showed more complete attack graphs.

IEEE International Conference on Cloud Computing Technology and Science · Oct 1

Mathematics & Statistics 1

Minyi Huang Mathematics & Statistics

Risk-Sensitive Linear-Quadratic-Gaussian Graphon Mean-Field Games

The paper develops a mathematical framework for very large networks of different agents who each want to minimize a cost while accounting for their own risk sensitivity. It shows that decentralized strategies can be defined and proves they form an approximate Nash equilibrium, under stated conditions, using new error estimates suited to the exponential cost, and it includes a numerical example.

SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization · Nov 19 · open access

Neuroscience 1

Amedeo D'Angiulli Neuroscience

Depth-of-Anesthesia Monitoring in Closed-Loop Anesthesia Delivery with Open BIS Validation and Neuromorphic Feasibility

The authors validated an open implementation of the bispectral index, a clinical measure of anesthesia depth derived from EEG, against reference data from VitalDB. They also evaluated simulated population drug response under a standard PID closed-loop controller and assessed whether the same controller could be realized in a neuromorphic framework, leaving larger-cohort validation and prospective testing for future work.

Applied Sciences · Nov 17 · open access

Physics 2

Dag Gillberg Physics, Jesse Heilman Physics with a large collaboration

Measurements of charged-particle pseudorapidity and transverse momentum distributions in O+O and Ne+Ne collisions at s N N = 5.36 TeV with the ATLAS detector

The ATLAS collaboration measured how many charged particles are produced, and their transverse momentum, in oxygen-oxygen and neon-neon collisions at 5.36 TeV, as a function of angle and collision centrality. The results are compared with hydrodynamic calculations, so they give reference data for testing models of these collisions.

Physical Review C · Nov 19 · open access · large collaboration
Simon Viel Physics, Razvan Gornea Physics with a large collaboration

nEXO radioassay program

The nEXO collaboration reports 300 material radioactivity assays, made with several low-background counting and mass spectrometry methods, to help select clean materials for the planned nEXO double-beta decay experiment. The compilation includes some of the tightest published limits on thorium-232 and uranium-238 content, and it can save other low-radioactivity experiments from repeating material studies.

Physical Review C · Nov 17 · large collaboration
