Biology 1

Ashkan Golshani Biology

Chromosome-scale reference assembly of New World lupin Lupinus mexicanus

The authors assembled a chromosome-scale reference genome for Lupinus mexicanus, a lupin native to central Mexico. The assembly spans 837 Mb on 24 chromosomes and includes 36,575 protein-coding genes. It gives researchers a resource for studying New World lupins, which are less studied than Old World species, as potential high-protein crops.

Zenodo (CERN European Organization for Nuclear Research) · Nov 2 · open access

Computer Science 1

Edward Melcer Computer Science

Comparing Game-Based and Virtual Objective Structured Clinical Examination Formats: Perceived Fairness and Achievement Emotions in Clinical Competency Examinations for Low- and Middle-Income Countries

This study compared a digital game-based assessment platform (ENTRUST) with virtual in-person-style clinical exams (OSCEs) during a high-stakes surgical certification exam for trainees across sub-Saharan Africa. Examinees reported lower anxiety, higher enjoyment and stronger perceptions of fairness with the game-based format, and the authors conclude that how an assessment is designed shapes how fair and stressful it feels.

Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction · Nov 3 · open access

Earth Sciences 1

Hillary Maddin Earth Sciences

Estimation of body-mass distributions of Carboniferous tetrapod communities and comparison with modern coastal tropical wetlands

The authors estimated body masses for 21 tetrapods from Pennsylvanian sites at Joggins and Point Aconi, Nova Scotia, using the surface area of vertebrae, and compared the size distributions with modern coastal tropical wetland communities. They conclude that bias in which animals get preserved explains some of the differences, and that the limited range of diets among Carboniferous tetrapods may explain others.

Paleobiology · Nov 4 · open access

Recommended

Game-based surgical exam rated fairer and less stressful than virtual OSCEs

Melcer's team compared the ENTRUST game-based platform with virtual OSCEs in a surgical certification exam. Examinees reported lower anxiety, more enjoyment and stronger perceptions of fairness with ENTRUST.

Route
Partnership. Stanford leads the platform and its authoring tools. Carleton's contribution is design and evaluation know-how, so this is not a clean licence.
Market
Certification bodies, medical boards and exam vendors already buy assessment delivery. Oral and OSCE exams are costly and resource intensive. ENTRUST is positioned as an alternative format.
Partners
  • Prometric (Baltimore, USA): Large exam vendor with a VR skills and simulation assessment offering.
  • Intuitive Surgical (USA): Already linked to ENTRUST through its surgical education learners forum.
Carleton lead
. Melcer (Computer Science) is first author and helped create the ENTRUST platform.
Evidence
ENTRUST was compared with virtual OSCEs in a real high-stakes surgical certification exam across sub-Saharan Africa.
Next step
Call Melcer and Dana Lin at Stanford first to settle who owns ENTRUST IP. Then approach an assessment vendor with a Mitacs Accelerate or NSERC Alliance proposal. Stanford is a co-owner.
