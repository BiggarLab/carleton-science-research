Biology 2

Bruce McKay Biology, Maria DeRosa Chemistry, David Sabatino Chemistrycross-unit

Uncovering the importance of lipofection for aptamer-mediated therapeutic oligonucleotide delivery in a HER2+ SKBR3 breast cancer cell line

The team built a HER2-targeting DNA aptamer joined to an RNA strand that can carry a therapeutic oligonucleotide into HER2-positive breast cancer cells. Their results show that how well the cargo gets in depends heavily on lipofection, which matters for anyone designing aptamer-based delivery.

Bioorganic & Medicinal Chemistry Letters · Sep 30 · open access
Nicolas Rodrigue Biology

Identifying patterns of selection at synonymous sites in a Pseudomonas phylogeny

DNA changes that leave the protein unchanged are usually assumed to be neutral. This study combines Bayesian substitution mapping with clustering to pick out the synonymous sites in two Pseudomonas genes that appear to be under selection anyway.

Genome Biology and Evolution · Sep 29 · open access

Chemistry 1

Katherine Marczenko Chemistry

Pendant Phosphonium Outperforms Ammonium by Strengthening Local Electric Fields in a Ruthenium Hydrogenation Catalyst

Swapping a pendant ammonium group for phosphonium in a ruthenium catalyst that turns CO2 into methanol raised both its turnover rate and its selectivity. The authors trace the gain to a stronger local electric field around the metal centre.

ACS Catalysis · Sep 30

Computer Science 3

Nadine Marie Moacdieh Computer Science

Measuring display clutter in different contexts: evidence from performance and attention in websites and maps

Several algorithms claim to measure how cluttered a screen is. Testing them against people's actual search speed and eye movements on websites and maps, only feature congestion tracked slower responses in both settings.

Ergonomics · Oct 1
AbdelRahman Abdou Computer Science

The Silent Spill: Measuring Sensitive Data Leaks Across Public URL Repositories

Services like VirusTotal, URLScan and the Wayback Machine publish huge numbers of URLs, and some of those links expose private information. The authors built an automated pipeline to find these leaks and ran it over roughly six million URLs to measure how common they are.

ACM Internet Measurement Conference · Sep 29 · open access

Mathematics & Statistics 1

Dave Campbell Mathematics & Statistics

Signal-Adapted Kernels and Lazy-Evaluation Gaussian Process Regression

Standard radial basis function regression tends to ripple on real data, and full Gaussian process regression gets slow on large datasets. This paper offers a non-stationary kernel with a lazy-evaluation scheme that avoids both problems while staying simple to implement.

Communications in Mathematics and Statistics · Sep 28 · open access

Neuroscience 1

Pediatric social robotics in ASD: navigating AI blind-spots toward clinical validation

A mini-review of three areas where social robots for autistic children are moving faster than the evidence: wearable sensors, VR and robot hybrids, and generative AI conversation. It argues for longer, larger clinical studies before these tools spread.

European Journal of Pediatrics · Sep 29 · open access

Label-free multimodal nonlinear optical imaging reveals key biomarkers of cellular radiation response

A dye-free imaging platform built on stimulated Raman scattering microscopy follows lipid and protein changes in single cells after radiation exposure, without destructive sample preparation.

Thomas Koffas Physics

Carrier dynamics in neutron irradiated high-quality epitaxial GaN

Using three optical probes, the authors tracked how neutron irradiation shortens charge-carrier lifetimes in gallium nitride, from about 200 nanoseconds down to picoseconds at high defect levels. The results matter for radiation-hard sensors and detectors.

Innovation and partnering 1 opportunity this week

Partnership: exposed-URL monitoring with AbdelRahman Abdou (Computer Science)

What. Abdou and Concordia co-authors built an automated system that finds sensitive data leaking through public URL repositories such as URLScan, VirusTotal and the Wayback Machine. Across about six million URLs it found over 12,000 likely exposures, including live password-reset links, API keys, login tokens and 2FA backup codes.

Why there is market fit. Companies already pay for exposure management: continuous monitoring of the open and dark web for leaked credentials and data. Their usual sources are leak sites, stealer logs and the dark web. This work adds a different one, public URL scanners and web archives, with a tested method and a large set of labelled findings.

Who to approach.

  • Flare (Montreal). Threat exposure management platform that monitors leak sites, stealer logs and the dark web for exposed credentials and data. Raised US$30M in Series B in December 2024. The closest fit, and in the same city as the Concordia co-authors.
  • Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Ottawa). Not a company, but a natural local partner for notifying affected Canadian organizations and for a public-interest angle.

Suggested next step. A short call with Abdou on whether he wants an industry partner. If yes, introduce him to Flare and scope a Mitacs Accelerate or NSERC Alliance project on continuous monitoring of URL repositories. Bring in Carleton's industry partnerships office early and coordinate with Concordia, since the work is shared.

Licensing note. The detection code is already public on GitHub, so this is a partnership and know-how play, not a licence. The data set was withheld and has value as part of a collaboration.

Also screened, nothing to act on yet: the ruthenium CO2-to-methanol catalyst (Marczenko) is early-stage homogeneous chemistry, and the HER2 aptamer delivery work (McKay, DeRosa, Sabatino) is still at the cell-line stage. Both are worth revisiting if follow-up papers move toward scale-up or animal data.
