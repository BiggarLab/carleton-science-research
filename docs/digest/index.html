Biology 3

Bruce McKay Biology, David Sabatino Chemistry, Maria DeRosa Chemistrycross-unit

Uncovering the importance of lipofection for aptamer-mediated therapeutic oligonucleotide delivery in a HER2+ SKBR3 breast cancer cell line

The team modified a DNA aptamer that targets the HER2 receptor so it could carry antisense or siRNA strands, then tested it in a HER2-positive breast cancer cell line. The construct bound to and entered the cells, and the siRNA version silenced the cancer survival protein GRP78, but only when a Lipofectamine transfection agent was used, so delivery of this type of genetic therapy still depends on that agent.

Bioorganic & Medicinal Chemistry Letters · Sep 30 · open access
Nicolas Rodrigue Biology

Identifying patterns of selection at synonymous sites in a Pseudomonas phylogeny

The authors used a statistical method on sequence comparisons to look for DNA changes that do not alter the protein but may still be under selection, focusing on two genes in Pseudomonas bacteria. About 8% and 7.5% of the relevant sites in the two genes were highly constrained, including some already shown in experiments to be beneficial, which suggests such changes may often contribute to adaptation.

Genome Biology and Evolution · Sep 29 · open access
Heath MacMillan Biology

Drivers of insect decline: direct, indirect, and combined pressures

This review pulls together the evidence on what is driving insect declines, organized around the IPBES framework and with statements of confidence so it can feed into biodiversity assessments. Land-use and climate change are well established as causes, while insecticides and light pollution do disproportionate harm but are less well understood, and the combined effect of multiple stressors remains unclear.

Annals of the Entomological Society of America · Sep 4 · open access

Chemistry 1

Katherine Marczenko Chemistry

Pendant Phosphonium Outperforms Ammonium by Strengthening Local Electric Fields in a Ruthenium Hydrogenation Catalyst

This study compared two versions of a ruthenium catalyst that converts carbon dioxide to methanol, one with a pendant phosphonium group and one with an ammonium group. The phosphonium version produced methanol faster and more selectively, and computational work indicated that it lowers the energy barrier of a key step by strengthening the local electric field.

Computer Science 3

Nadine Marie Moacdieh Computer Science

Measuring display clutter in different contexts: evidence from performance and attention in websites and maps

Participants searched for targets on websites and maps while the researchers compared three algorithms that score display clutter against response times and eye movements. Only feature congestion was linked to slower response times in both settings, and the findings give recommendations on which clutter and eye tracking measures to use for each type of display, with caution about some.

Ergonomics · Oct 1
AbdelRahman Abdou Computer Science

The Silent Spill: Measuring Sensitive Data Leaks Across Public URL Repositories

The authors built an automated system to find sensitive information in URLs that are publicly listed on security scanning services, web archives, and paste sites. Applied to about 6.1 million URLs, it flagged 12,331 potential exposures involving authentication, financial, personal, and document data, which shows that accidental leaks through these services are still occurring.

Mathematics & Statistics 1

Dave Campbell Mathematics & Statistics

Signal-Adapted Kernels and Lazy-Evaluation Gaussian Process Regression

The authors developed a Gaussian process regression method that adapts its kernel to the data and evaluates only the nearby training data for each prediction, which addresses both the oscillation problems of simpler models and the poor scaling to large datasets. The method has continuity guarantees for its predictions and was compared with other approaches on real rainfall and elevation datasets.

Communications in Mathematics and Statistics · Sep 28 · open access

Neuroscience 1

Amedeo D'Angiulli Neuroscience

Pediatric social robotics in ASD: navigating AI blind-spots toward clinical validation

This mini-review looks at social robots used with children who have autism, focusing on three areas with little validation so far: robots combined with wearable sensors, virtual reality hybrids, and generative AI conversation. The authors conclude that these systems cannot yet be supported as primary treatments, and they propose a five-part validation roadmap that includes multisite randomized trials and longitudinal monitoring.

European Journal of Pediatrics · Sep 29 · open access

Physics 2

Dag Gillberg Physics, Jesse Heilman Physics, Alain Bellerive Physics, Kevin Graham Physics, Thomas Koffas Physics with a large collaboration

Search for compressed electroweak SUSY production in final states with two $\tau$-leptons in $\sqrt{s} = 13$ and $13.6$ TeV $pp$ collisions with the ATLAS detector

This ATLAS collaboration paper searches Large Hadron Collider proton collision data for supersymmetric particles (tau-sleptons, charginos, and neutralinos) in events containing tau leptons. The results are generally consistent with the Standard Model, with a few deviations of 2.1 to 2.4 sigma, and limits were set on some particle masses, for example chargino masses up to 175 GeV for a 30 GeV mass splitting, but no limit was set for direct tau-slepton production when all channels were combined.

CERN Document Server (European Organization for Nuclear Research) · Sep 29 · large collaboration
Thomas Koffas Physics

Carrier dynamics in neutron irradiated high-quality epitaxial GaN

The authors used three measurement techniques to study how long charge carriers last in gallium nitride layers before and after neutron irradiation. Carrier lifetime fell from about 200 nanoseconds to about 2 picoseconds across the conditions tested as radiation defects increased, and the trapping energy of about 0.3 eV points to carbon-related defects as likely candidates for the main trapping centres.

Scientific Reports · Sep 29 · open access

Innovation and partnering no opportunities this week

Nothing this week cleared the bar for a real licensing or partnership opportunity.

Nothing qualifies this week. Near-misses: (1) The Abdou data-leak detection paper has a public arXiv preprint (CC BY 4.0) and its lead authors are at Concordia, so any route would be a partnership with shared ownership. The abstract also reports no accuracy or false-positive figures, which a buyer would ask for first. (2) The Marczenko Ru/phosphonium CO2-to-methanol catalyst is lab-scale homogeneous chemistry with no reported scale-up or product path. (3) The McKay/Sabatino/DeRosa HER2 aptamer work is cell-line only, and gene silencing depended on Lipofectamine, a transfection reagent used in cell culture. (4) The Koffas GaN neutron-irradiation study is device-physics characterization with no identified product or buyer. (5) The Campbell kernel/GPR method and the Moacdieh clutter-metric study are methods work with no identified product path. The remaining items are reviews or basic evolutionary analysis.
