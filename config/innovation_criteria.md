# Innovation and partnering section: rules

- Include an item only when it is a real, actionable opportunity. Empty weeks are expected and fine.
- Each item needs plausible market fit: an existing market or buyers for the result, not "could be useful someday".
- Name specific partner companies active in the space, small to large. Prefer Ottawa or Canadian companies. Verify each company exists and is currently active before naming it. A government lab or agency can be added as a second partner when it is a natural fit.
- Say whether the route is licensing or partnership. If code or methods are already public (GitHub, preprint), it is a partnership or know-how play, not a licence. Mention patent grace periods only when relevant (Canada and the US allow 12 months after the inventor's own disclosure; most of Europe allows none).
- Give one concrete next step: who to call, and which program fits (for example Mitacs Accelerate, NSERC Alliance, OCI, NRC IRAP for the company). Flag co-owners at other institutions.
- Early-stage work (cell-line only, lab-scale homogeneous chemistry, theory without a product path) goes in the one-line "also screened" note, with the reason it is not actionable yet.
